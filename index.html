Privacy Policy for TeamTweaker

Effective date: 2026-10-20
App: TeamTweaker (Android package: com.christianfds.teamtweaker)
Publisher / data controller: Christian Franchin (independent developer, “christianfds”)
Contact: christianfranchin.dev@gmail.com

This policy covers the TeamTweaker Android app on Google Play, the optional web build, and any future iOS release. It is written to meet the EU/UK GDPR, the Brazilian LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018), and the US state privacy laws described in section 10, including the CCPA/CPRA.

1. Summary

  • Your roster, teams, scores and settings live only on your device. There are no accounts and no servers of ours that store them.
  • Two third-party services keep the app free: Google AdMob for banner ads and RevenueCat for the one-time “remove ads” purchase.
  • Those services receive limited technical data: an advertising identifier, IP address, app/device information, ad interactions, and purchase status. They never receive your players’ names, ratings, lineups or match data.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we run no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

2. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter stays in on-device storage (AsyncStorage) unless you explicitly export it:

  • player names, star ratings and availability flags
  • team count / size settings, team naming mode, custom team names, pins
  • balancing results, scoreboard fixture and scores, scheduler match log
  • language, theme and winning-target settings

The session is stored under teamtweaker:current:v1. Clearing app data, using Start over, or uninstalling the app deletes it. We hold no server copy and cannot retrieve or delete it for you.

The remove-ads entitlement is not kept as a trusted local flag: the app re-checks it with RevenueCat and the store on launch.

4. Advertising, advertising identifiers and tracking

  • Banners appear at the bottom of the four main tabs. They are never shown in fullscreen Present mode.
  • Personalized ads. Where permitted, Google may use your advertising identifier and ad interactions across apps and sites to select and measure ads. Where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US states with opt-in rules) the app shows a Google-certified consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before requesting personalized ads.
  • Non-personalized ads. If you decline, or if a consent state cannot be established, the app requests non-personalized ads only. These are selected from contextual information and are not based on your advertising identifier for profiling.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (typically Settings → Google → Ads). Deleting it disables the advertising ID for all apps.
  • iOS / IDFA. No iOS version is published yet. When ads ship on iOS, the app will request App Tracking Transparency permission before any IDFA access. Declining means no IDFA, no cross-app tracking, and non-personalized ads only; you can change the choice later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • We ourselves never receive your advertising identifier or IP address; they are exchanged directly between your device and Google.

5. Purchases

The optional one-time “remove ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. RevenueCat validates the receipt and tells the app whether the entitlement is active, so banners disappear on every device where the purchase is restored. We receive no payment details. Purchase records are retained as long as needed to honour and restore the purchase and to meet tax and accounting duties.

6. What we never receive

  • players’ names, ratings or availability
  • team, score, fixture, scheduler or match history data
  • export or backup content
  • your advertising identifier or IP address
  • payment card details

7. Sharing and international transfers

We share personal data only with the services in section 3, and only for the purposes listed there. We do not sell personal information and do not disclose it to anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Google and RevenueCat are based in the United States. Where personal data leaves the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or Brazil, those providers state that they rely on appropriate safeguards — Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework — as described in their published policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

8. Retention

  • On-device data: until you clear it in-app, clear app storage, or uninstall.
  • Ad data at Google: according to Google’s retention policies.
  • RevenueCat: for as long as needed to maintain and restore your purchase.

9. Legal bases (GDPR / LGPD)

  • Consent — personalized advertising, and any optional tracking on iOS. You may withdraw at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Legitimate interests — serving non-personalized ads, keeping the app free, security, fraud prevention, and diagnosing failures. We balance these against your rights and use the minimum data necessary.
  • Contract — validating and restoring the remove-ads purchase you chose to buy.
  • Legal obligation — retaining purchase records where tax or accounting law requires it.

Under the LGPD the same bases apply (Art. 7): consent, legitimate interest, performance of a contract, and legal obligation.

10. Your rights

10.1 EU / UK GDPR

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format, as well as the right to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Because your team data never leaves your device, most requests are satisfied by clearing app data or uninstalling.

10.2 Brazil (LGPD)

Under Art. 18 you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about sharing, and revocation of consent. You may also complain to the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).

10.3 United States (CCPA/CPRA and other state laws)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information; where personalized advertising counts as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under your state’s law, you can opt out as described in section 11. Residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws (for example Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon) have equivalent rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy, and opt out of targeted advertising.

10.4 How to exercise your rights

Email christianfranchin.dev@gmail.com. We will respond within the period the applicable law requires (normally 30 days, or 15 days under the LGPD). We may need to ask for information sufficient to identify the request, but we hold no account system, so in practice the fastest route for team data is your own device. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, you can also contact them directly, or ask us and we will pass the request on.

11. Do Not Sell or Share

We do not sell personal information for money. To opt out of personalized advertising:

  1. decline personalized ads in the consent message when it is shown, or
  2. change your choice later through the privacy options the consent message offers, and/or
  3. reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (Android), or turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track (iOS).

In each case the app continues to work and continues to show non-personalized ads unless you buy remove ads.

12. Children

TeamTweaker is a general-audience organizer tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA), under 16 (GDPR Art. 8, where applicable), or under 12 (LGPD Art. 14). Player names you type — which may include minors — stay on your device and are never sent to us. If you believe a child’s data has reached a third-party service through this app, contact us and we will help you exercise deletion rights.

13. Security

Team data is protected by your device’s own security (screen lock, OS sandbox, app storage permissions) and encryption in transit for the third-party SDKs. No method is perfectly secure, but we keep the data surface deliberately small: no accounts, no server of ours, no analytics.

14. Changes

Material changes are published at the hosted copy above with a new effective date before the corresponding app update ships. The date at the top of this page always identifies the current version.
