Privacy Policy for TeamTweaker

Effective date: 2026-10-15
App: TeamTweaker (Android package: com.christianfds.teamtweaker)
Publisher: christianfds
Contact: christianfranchin.dev@gmail.com

1. Overview

TeamTweaker creates balanced teams from player star ratings and runs a local match scoreboard. The app works fully on your device. There are no accounts, no servers operated by us, and no data sent to us.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter stays in on-device storage (AsyncStorage) unless you explicitly export it:

  • player names, star ratings, and availability flags
  • team count / size settings, team naming mode, custom team names, pins
  • balancing results, scoreboard fixture and scores, scheduler match log
  • language, theme, and remove-ads preference flags

Storage keys include teamtweaker:current:v1 and teamtweaker.premium.v1. Clearing app data, using Start over, or uninstalling the app deletes it. We hold no server copy and cannot retrieve or delete it for you.

3. Other people’s data

Player names may identify teammates, including minors. That information never leaves your device through us. Only add names you are entitled to use, and only share exports (Share sheet / clipboard) with consent.

4. Data we do not collect

In this version we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, location, contacts, photos, identifiers, or usage analytics. The app ships without analytics, crash-reporting, login, or advertising SDKs.

  • Device locale is read on-device to pick the default language only.
  • Share / Copy sends team text through the OS only when you tap it.
  • Opening an external link uses an in-app browser; the target site’s own policy then applies.

5. Advertising and tracking

This version serves no third-party ads, uses no advertising ID, and performs no cross-app tracking. Banner slots in the app render nothing in production builds.

If a future version enables Google AdMob (Android/iOS) or AdSense (web), Google and its partners may collect device identifiers, approximate IP-based location, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads. This policy will be updated before such a release, and the Play Data safety declaration will be changed accordingly. On iOS only, a future ads-supported version may request App Tracking Transparency permission; denying it only results in non-personalized ads.

6. Purchases

A future one-time “remove ads” purchase may be offered through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). Payments are processed by the store; we only keep a local on-device flag that ads are removed.

7. Children

TeamTweaker is a general-audience organizer tool and is not directed at children. Because all entered data stays on the organizer’s device, we do not centrally collect children’s data.

8. Retention and your rights

Retention is under your control since data never leaves your device: clear it in-app, clear app storage in system settings, or uninstall. For questions, contact christianfranchin.dev@gmail.com.

9. Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date before the corresponding app update ships.
